kryfo is a privacy-first messenger.

no phone numbers. no emails. no accounts. nothing that ties a kryfo to you.

you pair with a friend by scanning a QR code in person, or sharing a kryfo:// link. each kryfo is a three-word identity like "thumb-behave-boring" - easy to read out, impossible to guess.

messages are end-to-end encrypted using the Signal Double Ratchet (the same protocol Signal and WhatsApp use). they travel over Tor onion routing by default, so your network sees only encrypted Tor traffic - not who you're talking to. when one side is offline, messages wait on Nostr relays as encrypted opaque blobs.

what kryfo includes:
- 1:1 chat with end-to-end encryption
- ghost mode - messages that delete themselves after 30 seconds to 24 hours
- reactions and replies
- app lock with PIN + fingerprint
- panic PIN that silently wipes the app (looks like a crash)
- self-generated avatars from your kryfo ID
- a clean editorial design - no neon banners, no clutter

what kryfo doesn't include:
- no telemetry, crash reporting, or analytics - ever
- no Google Play Services
- no proprietary blobs
- no contact list scraping
- no ads, no upsells

inspired by GrapheneOS (restraint over excitement), Signal (the protocol), and SimpleX (the architecture).

pre-alpha. solo dev, open source. usable for technical testers; not yet ready for everyday use.
