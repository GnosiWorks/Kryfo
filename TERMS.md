## No account

You never sign up. Kryfo does not ask for your phone number, your email address or your name, and your identity and messages live on your phone.

Our servers keep no accounts, no connection logs, no IP addresses, no contact lists and no member lists. They carry encrypted messages that nobody can read, for up to 14 days so a phone that was offline can catch up, and the public handles people choose to claim. The [privacy notice](https://kryfo.app/privacy) says exactly what each server sees.

## Fair use

Use Kryfo within the law where you are. Do not use our servers to attack, flood or overload them, or anyone else.

Public listings, such as handles and their short descriptions, can be seen by anyone, so they must not contain anything illegal. They are the only thing we moderate. A listing that breaks the law or these terms is removed, and reports go to the address in the [impressum](https://kryfo.app/impressum).

Your chats are encrypted on your phone. We cannot read them, so we do not moderate them, and you are responsible for what you send.

## Tips on Google Play

The Google Play version of Kryfo lets you leave a tip to support the work. A tip is a payment you choose to make. It gives you a supporter badge and nothing else: everything in Kryfo is free for everyone, with or without one.

Tips are paid through Google Play, under Google Play's terms. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card details. Refunds follow [Google Play's refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637), so ask Google Play for one.
