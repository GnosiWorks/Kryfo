# Code of conduct

Kryfo is made for people who need to talk safely, so the place where it is built should feel safe too.

## What I ask

- Be kind and patient, also when you disagree. Argue about the idea, not the person.
- Many people here write in their second or third language. Read generously and write plainly.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't post anyone's identity, contact details, location or messages without their consent, and never try to unmask a Kryfo user.
- No harassment, insults, threats, sexual content, or attacks on anyone for who they are or where they live.
- No spam and no advertising.

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and the developer chat in the app.

## If something goes wrong

Tell me privately: gnosiworks@proton.me, or the "Marios · built Kryfo" chat in the app. I read every report, keep the reporter's name to myself, and reply within a week.

Depending on what happened I may ask someone to stop, hide or delete comments, or block someone from the project, for a while or for good.

The spirit of this follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
