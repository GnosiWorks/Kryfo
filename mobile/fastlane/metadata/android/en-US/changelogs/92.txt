The wipe did not wipe. Both the wipe pin and the settings wipe deleted messages and keys but lost a race on the way out, so the next launch asked for the old pin and showed a fresh identity. The wipe now goes through Android's own clear-data call: everything gone, next launch is onboarding.

The app lock only covered the home screen. Leave from settings or a chat and come back, and the pin never showed until you walked home. Fixed: the lock covers every screen. A screenshot no longer asks for the pin afterwards.

A message to someone who has not added you back could show a tick while it sat at an address they never read. It now says it is waiting for them and keeps trying. A photo stuck at 98% resumes from the missing piece instead of starting over, and the receiving side says paused instead of vanishing.

Sentence case throughout, shorter settings hints, no white flash on the screenshot toggle, open source row copies the link instead of opening a browser.
