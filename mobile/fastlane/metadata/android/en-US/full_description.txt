Halo is a private messenger built for people who don't want to be counted,
profiled, or looked up.

There is no phone number, no email, no account. You are a random ID and a
key pair, generated on your device. Nobody — including us — can search for
you, list you, or connect your conversations to a real identity.

Every message travels through Tor. Content is end-to-end encrypted with the
Signal protocol, and the transport is designed so the network itself learns
as little as possible: no central server holds your contact graph, delivery
falls back across relays and onion routes, and there is no address book
upload, ever.

FEATURES

* No phone number or email to sign up
* End-to-end encryption (Signal protocol) with self-healing sessions
* All traffic routed through an embedded Tor client
* Direct onion delivery, with relay fallback when a peer is offline
* Group chats with photos, files, voice messages, replies, reactions and pins
* Ghost mode: messages that burn after a set time
* Link previews fetched by the sender, so the receiver never touches the origin
* Encrypted local database and encrypted backups
* App lock with biometrics or PIN
* Panic wipe that removes everything, including media on disk
* No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry of any kind
* No ads, no investors, nothing to sell

FUNDING

Halo takes no venture money and shows no ads. It is funded entirely by the
people who use it. Backing is optional, anonymous, and never required —
every feature works the same whether you give anything or not. Privacy is
not behind a paywall.
