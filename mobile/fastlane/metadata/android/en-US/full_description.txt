a private messenger. no phone number, no email, no account. you are a key
pair generated on your device. nobody, including us, can look you up or tie
your chats to a real identity.

everything travels over tor. content is end to end encrypted with the signal
protocol. when a contact is offline, messages wait on relays, sealed so the
relay learns nothing about who is talking or what is said. no address book
upload, ever.

features:

* no phone number or email to sign up
* end to end encryption (signal protocol)
* all traffic through an embedded tor client
* direct onion delivery, relay fallback when a peer is offline
* groups with photos, files, voice messages, replies, reactions, pins and polls
* animated stickers that travel as a name, so no picture leaves the phone
* search across all your chats, kept in the encrypted database
* timed messages that burn after a set time
* encrypted local database and encrypted backups
* app lock with pin or biometrics, a decoy pin that opens an empty kryfo, and hidden chats behind a pin of their own
* panic wipe that removes everything, including media on disk
* a chat with the person who builds kryfo, anonymous if you like
* 15 languages
* no analytics, no telemetry, no ads

funding: no ads, no investors. it runs on what backers give. backing is
optional and anonymous, and every feature works the same either way.
