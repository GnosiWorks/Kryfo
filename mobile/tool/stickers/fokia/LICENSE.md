# Fokia sticker art

The Fokia stickers: the SVG masters in `svg/`, their motion in `anim.txt`, and
`mobile/assets/stickers/fokia.kst`, which is built from them.

The SVG files are the masters. The pack and the PNG renders are made from them
by `mobile/tool/pack_stickers.py`. The PNGs are rendered in the build and are
not kept in the repository.

## Names

The licence covers the art, not the names. "Kryfo" is the name of this app and
"Fokia" the name of its sticker pack. A fork may use and change the art under
CC BY-SA 4.0, but may not use the names Kryfo or Fokia for itself or its
stickers.
