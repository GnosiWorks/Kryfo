# Fokia Remix sticker art

The Fokia Remix stickers: the SVG masters in `svg/`, their motion in
`anim.txt`, and `mobile/assets/stickers/fokiaremix.kst`, which is built from
them.

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The SVG files are the masters. The pack and the PNG renders are made from them
by `mobile/tool/pack_stickers.py --pack fokiaremix`. The PNGs are rendered in
the build and are not kept in the repository.

The English words in stickers 41 and 47 are part of the drawing, not text to
translate.

## Names

The licence covers the art, not the names. "Kryfo" is the name of this app and
"Fokia" the name of its stickers and their character, used in "Fokia" and
"Fokia Remix". A fork may use and change the art under CC BY-SA 4.0, but may
not use the names Kryfo or Fokia for itself or its stickers.
