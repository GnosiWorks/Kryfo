# Governance

Kryfo is founded and maintained by Marios (GnosiWorks, Berlin).

## Who decides
Marios makes the final call on the roadmap, releases and what gets merged.

## Who helps
Two volunteers help with testing, translations and UI feedback.
Anyone can contribute through issues and pull requests.

## Contributions
Every outside contribution is reviewed before it goes in.
If it's merged, the contributor stays credited as the author.

## Security reports
Please report security issues privately, see SECURITY.md.
Fixes ship in a release before any details are made public.

## Money
Donations and grants go through our Open Collective, hosted by
Open Source Europe: https://opencollective.com/kryfo
